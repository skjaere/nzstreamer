# Contributing

Pull requests are welcome once you have signed the [Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md) (CLA).

## Why a CLA

NZStreamer is public under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, which allows noncommercial use only. The CLA lets the maintainer also use your contribution commercially, for example under another licence.

## How to sign

1. Open your pull request. On your first one, a bot comments with a link to the CLA and a sentence to sign with.
2. Read the CLA. To sign it, post a new comment on the pull request that holds only that sentence.
3. The bot records your signature, and the CLA check passes. If it doesn't, comment `recheck`.

Signing once covers your later pull requests too.

The bot matches your commits to your GitHub account by their e-mail address. Commit with an address your account has (a verified one, or its noreply address), or the bot can't tell that you signed.

## How pull requests are merged

Each pull request is merged by squash: it lands on main as one commit, with you as its author. Please give the pull request a Conventional Commits title, such as `fix: ...` or `feat: ...`. The squash commit takes it as its subject, and the release notes are made from those subjects.
