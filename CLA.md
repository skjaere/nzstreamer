# Contributor Licence Agreement

Version 1.0. This text has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

This agreement is between you, the person who signs it, and the maintainer of this repository, who holds the GitHub account skjaere (the "maintainer"). It covers every contribution you submit to this repository.

## 1. Words used here

- A "contribution" is any code, text, image or other material that you submit to this repository, and any change to it that you submit later.
- To "submit" is to send through GitHub to this repository, in a pull request, its commits or its suggested changes.

## 2. What you keep

You keep the copyright and every other right you have in your contribution, and you may use it in any way you like. This agreement gives the maintainer rights beside yours. It takes none away from you.

## 3. The rights you give the maintainer

For each contribution, you give the maintainer the right to use, copy, change, translate, combine with other work, publish and distribute it, and to make it available to the public, in every type of use that is known when you sign. This includes:

- using it under licences other than this repository's own, open or closed;

This right is:

- non-exclusive: you, and anyone you choose, may have the same rights;
- worldwide;
- royalty-free: no payment is due for it, now or later;
- irrevocable: you can't withdraw it once you have submitted the contribution;
- transferable: the maintainer may pass it on to someone else;
- sublicensable: the maintainer may give others any of these rights.

This agreement grants no right in a type of use that is unknown when you sign.

If you own or control a patent that your contribution infringes, on its own or combined with the work in this repository it was submitted to, you give the maintainer, and everyone who receives that work from the maintainer, a licence under that patent. It lets them make, use, sell, offer for sale, import and otherwise distribute your contribution, on its own and as part of that work. The licence is non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free and irrevocable.

## 5. What you confirm

Each time you submit a contribution, you confirm that:

- it is your own work, or you have the right to submit it under this agreement;
- if your employer, or anyone else, has rights in your work, they have allowed you to submit it under this agreement;
- you have named, in the pull request, every part of it that is not your own work, with where it comes from and its licence.

If one of these stops being true, you will tell the maintainer on GitHub, for example in the pull request.

## 6. No obligation

The maintainer doesn't have to use, merge or publish any contribution, and may remove one later.

## 7. No warranty

Apart from what you confirm in section 5, you give your contribution as it is, with no warranty of any kind. You don't have to support it.

## 8. How you sign, and the record kept

You sign by posting, on a pull request to this repository, a comment that holds only the sentence the CLA bot gives. Your signature covers all your contributions to this repository, those already submitted and those you submit later, under this version of the agreement.

The record of your signature is kept in public, on this repository's `cla-signatures` branch. It holds your GitHub login, your GitHub account's id, the id and time of your signing comment, this repository's id and the pull request's number. It is kept as long as contributions it covers are used, since it shows that you signed.

## 9. Later versions

A changed agreement gets a new version number. Your signature stays with the version you signed. A later version applies to you only once you sign it.
