# One entry a line: kind<TAB>path glob<TAB>regex<TAB># why. kind is home, lan, email or key. The glob is matched
# against the file's path, * spanning folders; the regex must match the whole address (or a key file's whole path), in
# any case. Every entry says why it may stay. Keep each as narrow as its files allow, and never add a real address.
